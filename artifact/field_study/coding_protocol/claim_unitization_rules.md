# Claim Unitization Rules

A claim unit is one paper-authored comparative sentence or semantically equivalent group of sentences with the same baselines, metric, direction, and problem scope.

Semantic duplicates are collapsed. Distinct metrics, distinct problem families, or distinct comparator pairs remain separate claims.

Accepted if the source authors report it as a comparison of two approaches on a reported metric or outcome. Secondary comparisons that meet this test are kept. Equivalence statements count (8 of the 455).

Chain: 630 candidates → 628 verbatim-verified → 457 extraction records (deduplication and acceptance) → 455 accepted (two source-grounded corrections). The row-level 630/628 sets and the 628 → 457 worksheet are not archived.
